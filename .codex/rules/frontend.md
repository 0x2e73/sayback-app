# Frontend

- Light and dark follow the system or the user's choice (`Settings.appearance`, applied by the main process through `nativeTheme.themeSource`; the renderer has no theme switch of its own). Translucent left pane over vibrancy, opaque right card, one accent, one primary action per screen. Both themes are read from the same tokens: a screen that works in one theme only is not finished.
- Every colour, size, radius, space and duration is a token declared in `src/renderer/styles.css`; feature CSS holds layout only, no literal, no fallback value, no undeclared token. Light values sit on `:root`, dark values in the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block, contrast reinforcements in the `prefers-contrast: more` blocks. A new colour token is declared in both themes and checked for contrast. `src/renderer/design-tokens.test.ts` enforces colours, type, radii and gaps and the contrast of the text, status and action pairs.
- Share `Button`, `Notice`, `Dialog`, `ConfirmDialog`, `Select`, `Segmented`, `Switch`, `Chip`, `SearchField`, `EmptyState`, `Skeleton`, `StatusBadge` and `LevelMeter` from `src/renderer/components/ui.tsx`; look for an existing one before writing another; extract on a real second use; screen-specific layout and copy stay in the owning feature. One stylesheet per component or feature in `src/renderer/styles/`; `styles.css` holds tokens, base rules, keyframes and reduced motion.
- Semantic HTML, labelled controls, keyboard support, visible focus. Forms keep their send button inactive while nothing changed and busy while the request runs. Every screen has real loading (skeleton), empty, error and ready states.
- Motion is CSS only, on the motion tokens of `src/renderer/styles.css`: durations `--duration-fast` (colours, hover, press), `-exit` (every exit, always quicker than an entrance), `-base`, `-slow`, `-row`, `-glide`, `-loop`, delays `--delay-1..3` and `--delay-wait`, curves `--ease-out`, `--ease-move`, `--ease-linear`, `--ease-ambient`, offsets `--motion-rise`, `-drop`, `-slide`, `-scale-in`, `-scale-out`, `-press`, `-press-soft`, `-icon`, `-accept`. Only `opacity`, `transform` and colours are animated; the one `height` transition is the sliding selection highlight of the list (`styles/meeting-list.css`). Entrances are transitions from `@starting-style`; exits are a `[data-state='closing']` rule on `--duration-exit`, driven by `usePresence` / `Presence` (`components/use-presence.ts`), which unmounts at the end of the opacity transition and makes the element `inert`; a dialog or popover that can leave is given `open` instead of being unmounted. Press is `scale(var(--motion-press))` on buttons and `--motion-press-soft` on chips, tabs, options and rows, at `--duration-fast`; `:hover` styles sit in `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Selection and the segmented thumb glide from one place to another, but jump on first placement and on resize. Keyboard-driven changes are instant. Keyframes (`spin`, `skeleton-pulse`, `chip-accept`, `turn-target`) and every reduced-motion rule live in `styles.css` only: reduced motion zeroes the `--motion-*` offsets and `--delay-*` tokens, so entrances become fades, and turns off loops and gliding. `src/renderer/motion-guard.test.ts` enforces it.
- Two slow ambient animations have their own tokens besides the motion durations: `--duration-ambient` (1.6 s) for the skeleton pulse and the flash on a passage reached from a note, and `--duration-finalize` (1.6 s) for the indeterminate « Finalisation… » line of the recording screen. Both are static under reduced motion (the passage stays highlighted, the line becomes a still bar).
- Concise French copy, no slogans, invented statistics or implementation jargon (endpoints, binary names and identifiers belong to the local guide); show real loading, empty, recording, processing, error and ready states. Notes come first; transcript, video and clarifications share timestamps.
- The island under the notch is the one exception to the theme: black and dark whatever the theme (`--color-island` and the `--color-island-*` tokens it rebinds), its shell alone animates `width`, `height` and `border-radius` on `--ease-island` and `--duration-island`; under reduced motion only its content fades. Its sizes come from the main process through the declared `--island-*` tokens.
